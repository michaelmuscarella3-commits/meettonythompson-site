THIS WEEK’S JOURNAL

65% of new business comes from referrals.

 

Are you doing everything you can to unlock them?

Early in my career, I treated a good introduction like the finish line. I’d get the name, say thank you, and move on. I was leaving so much on the table.

Then I learned that one introduction, handled well, can turn into five. It all comes down to how you run the first meeting.

 

How to Turn One Intro Into Five

 

You
(reader)
What You Do in the First Meeting

Person A
1

Give before you ask.

↳I walk in looking for a way to help, not a way to get.
🤝
 
 
2

Make their world the whole conversation.

↳I ask about their goals and their problems — and I listen.
💬
 
 
3

Offer a gift they can use today.

↳A useful idea, a helpful contact, or a quick answer to something they’re stuck on.
🎁
 
 
4

Ask one simple question.

↳“Is there anyone else in your world who would find what I do helpful?”
❓
 
 
5

Hand them a short note they can forward.
✍️
 
Make it easy, and one intro opens five doors.
Where the Intros Come From
 
👤

A peer with the same problem
 
👤

Someone on their team
 
👤

A trusted vendor or partner
 
👤

A former colleague
 
👤

A friend in their network

  
 
 

After training hundreds of professionals, I can tell you this holds up — it doesn’t matter what industry you’re in.

People help you when you help them first.
