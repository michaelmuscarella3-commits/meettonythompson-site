THIS WEEK’S JOURNAL

Most leaders think their team has a performance problem.

 

Most of the time, it’s a leadership problem wearing a performance costume.

 

Leadership Is a Mirror, Not a Megaphone

 

Average leaders point at the scoreboard.

Elite leaders:

•  Look in the mirror first

•  Own the gap before they assign it

•  Ask “what did I model?” before “what did they miss?”

Because they know:

Your team doesn’t rise to your expectations. They rise to your example.

 

If Your Team Feels Stuck…

 

It’s not a talent problem.

It’s a leadership ceiling.

•  No clear vision.

•  No consistent presence.

•  No real investment in their growth.

That’s where teams plateau — quietly, then all at once.

 

The Shift

 

Stop asking:

“Why isn’t my team stepping up?”

Start asking:

“Why am I leading from comfort instead of conviction?”
